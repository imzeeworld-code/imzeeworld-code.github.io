Who I Am

About Me

I'm Henry Tallen, an independent app developer operating under the brand World of Discoveries. I build fully functional Android applications entirely from my phone — no desktop, no formal coding background, just vision, creativity and determination.

I specialize in crafting AI-powered tools, voice assistants, music players, browsers, and productivity apps — all designed with a distinctive dark, gold-accented aesthetic and polished user experience.

Every app I release starts as a single idea and becomes a complete product ready for the Play Store.
